Controls

Browser: drag to look · WASD walk · scroll to zoom · ←→ change slide · C back to center · H hide panel. Drop images anywhere to load them.

Quest 2: trigger = next slide · grip = previous · left thumbstick = glide around the room · click a thumbstick = return to center. You can also walk physically.

Notes on accuracy

The wall is modeled as a cylinder with the image wrapped once around it. Projector brightness, color, and edge blending are not simulated, and the screen's height and bottom edge are estimates. Confirm them with library_hightechspaces@ncsu.edu.

Export each slide at the full 15,360 px width if you can. Lower-resolution exports will look softer here than in the room.
